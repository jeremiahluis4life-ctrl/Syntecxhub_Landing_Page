FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Send one message before Sunday and the next bag won't ship. There's no minimum number of deliveries.

Which areas do you deliver to?

All of Lagos, Mainland and Island. Outside Lagos we ship by courier for a fee shown at checkout.

I don't have a grinder. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Tell us how you brew and we grind it fresh on the day we pack your bag.

How should I store the coffee?

Keep the bag sealed, in a cupboard away from heat. Skip the fridge, since it adds moisture.
